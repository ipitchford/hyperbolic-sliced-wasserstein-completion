## Notation

Real hyperbolic space H^d, d >= 2, fixed origin o, finite p >= 1. Klein ball B^d (geodesics are straight chords), closed
ball with boundary sphere S^{d-1}. For a unit direction v, the geodesic projection coordinate is F_v(z) = atanh(v . z)
(Lemma 2.1). GHSW_p(P,Q)^p = average over v (uniform on S^{d-1}) of W_p^p of the one-dimensional pushforwards; D_p is its
extension to P(closed ball). C_{d,p} = p-th absolute moment of the density c_d sech^{d-1}(t); C_{d,2} = (1/2) psi_1((d-1)/2).

## Claims and where they are proved

| ID | Claim | Location | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Projection coordinate in the Klein model is atanh(v . z) | Lemma 2.1 | written proof + 36 numerical instances |
| C2 | Uniform angular envelope: sup over the closed ball of the angular p-th moment of F_v equals C_{d,p}, attained on the boundary | Lemma 2.2 | written proof + quadrature of six quadratic constants |
| C3 | GHSW_p extends with no moment assumption to D_p on P(closed Klein ball); the completion is that compact space; D_p metrises weak convergence; exact diameter 2 C_{d,p}^{1/p}; no control of hyperbolic transport moments | Theorem 2.3 | written proof (separation step credited to Lyons 2014) |
| C4 | Proposition 19 of Bonet, Drumetz and Courty (JMLR 2025), that the backprojection maps C_0 to C_0, fails: g(t,v) = sech^2 t gives a backprojection tending to 1 - 1/d at infinity | Proposition 3.1 | written proof + 12 numerical evaluations |
| C5 | Three exact radial regimes for D_p(delta_o, point at distance r): e^{-(d-1)r}, r e^{-2pr}, e^{-2pr} according to d-1 <, =, > 2p, with explicit constants | Theorem 4.1, (4.7) | written proof + 24 numerical cases |
| C6 | Depth insensitivity: points far out in the same direction are exponentially close in D_p | Corollary 4.2 | consequence of C5 |
| C7 | Boundary-sphere metric: beta_2(alpha) = pi alpha (d = 2); exact Legendre series with (1/2) alpha^2 log(1/alpha) behaviour (d = 3); comparable to alpha^2 with limit constant (d-2)/((d-1)(d-3)) (d >= 4) | Proposition 4.3, Theorem 4.4 | written proof + dilogarithm comparisons, Legendre coefficients, quadrature trends |
| C8 | Distribution-free sampling bound E D_p^p(P_n, P) <= S_{d,p} n^{-1/2} for every P, and a high-probability version | Theorem 5.1 | written proof + exact binomial identity |
| C9 | Minimax rate n^{-1/2} for D_p^p and n^{-1/(2p)} for D_p (constants not sharp; ratio about 20-80) | Theorem 5.2, Corollary 5.3 | written proof |
| C10 | Random-projection estimator unbiased with variance bound for each fixed pair (not uniform over pairs) | Proposition 5.4 | written proof |
| C11 | Adding a radial W_p term gives a complete metric with the hyperbolic Wasserstein W_p topology | Theorem 6.1 | written proof |
| C12 | Finitely many directions never separate laws, even with the radial law added | Proposition 6.2 | written proof |
| C13 | Not claimed: a new injectivity theorem; results for data-dependent origins, other angular measures, the horospherical construction or general Cartan-Hadamard manifolds; sharp minimax constants | Sections 1 and 7 | statement of scope |
